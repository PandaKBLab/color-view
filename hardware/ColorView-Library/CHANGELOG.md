# Changes

## 0.2.0

- Five devices in both EasyEDA and KiCad, including the separate HC-FPC-05-10-5RLTAG FPC connector.
- FPC connector includes five signal contacts and two mounting tabs, with a STEP model.
- Power silk uses VCC. VCC supply options are regulated 3.3 V or 5 V; signal logic remains 3.3 V.
- Models and import instructions are supplied with both packages. Export-machine paths are removed from the public EasyEDA archive.
