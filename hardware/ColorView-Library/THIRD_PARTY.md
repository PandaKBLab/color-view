# Connector asset source

The HC-FPC-05-10-5RLTAG connector pad geometry and STEP model are derived from the EasyEDA system-library entry for HCTL HC-FPC-05-10-5RLTAG (LCSC C5213728). This is a keyboard-side connector, separate from the Color View module installation model.

Source: [EasyEDA](https://pro.lceda.cn/) · [Connector information](https://item.szlcsc.com/datasheet/HC-FPC-05-10-5RLTAG/5909020.html).

The connector model retains its original header and attribution. Third-party asset rights remain with their respective owners. The two Color View module models show only simplified exterior and clearance geometry.
